    📜 Scoring rules

    Before the window opens, each player locks 10 ranked picks — the films they believe will gross the most at the domestic box office between May 1 and Sep 7, 2026, in predicted finish order — plus 3 dark horses, unordered. All 13 titles must be distinct. Only films released inside the window count, and only money earned through Labor Day counts.
